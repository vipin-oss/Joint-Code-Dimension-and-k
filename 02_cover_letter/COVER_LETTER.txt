COVER LETTER
============

To the Editor,
Finite Fields and Their Applications

Dear Editor,

We submit the enclosed manuscript, "Joint Code-Dimension and k-Galois-Hull
Enumerators for Repeated-Root Constacyclic Codes over Square-Free Affine
Algebras", by Arun Kumar Yadav, Mina Kumari, Mohd Vasiulla and Vipin Gupta, for
consideration as a research article in Finite Fields and Their Applications.
The manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration
by any other journal; all authors have approved the submission.

What the paper does. For all A-linear lambda-constacyclic codes of a fixed
repeated-root length over a square-free affine algebra over a finite field, we
determine the exact joint distribution of the code dimension and the k-Galois
hull dimension. The Frobenius identity writes every component modulus as a
p^t-th power of a square-free kernel, so a code is an exponent selection on the
distinct kernel factors. The k-Galois dual then acts through a
Frobenius-twisted reciprocal permutation, and on each of its orbits the hull
dimension is a cyclic sum of pairwise minima. The bivariate law of one orbit is
the trace of a power of an explicit weighted transfer matrix; multiplying over
orbits, with a dimension-only factor for components whose twist is
incompatible, gives a bivariate enumerator whose coefficients are the exact
multiplicities.

Main contributions.
1. The exact joint enumerator, valid for every twist, including incompatible
   components, with the per-code hull formula recovered as a special case.
2. The exact support of attainable (code dimension, hull dimension) pairs on
   one orbit, a trapezoid that includes a lower bound invisible from
   dimensions alone.
3. Closed means, variances and conditional laws, and the numbers of LCD,
   self-orthogonal, dual-containing and self-dual codes; the LCD count is
   independent of the multiplicity in the fully compatible case.
4. An algorithm polynomial in the length, the multiplicity and the number of
   kernel factors, verified against finite-field linear algebra in
   characteristics 2 and 3 and evaluated for all 3^31 codes of length 62 over
   F_32.

Relation to the literature. For a single code, Galois hull dimensions of
constacyclic codes over a field and over an affine algebra are known, and
average hull dimensions have been computed. The contribution here is the full
joint law over a family together with its enumerative consequences. The paper
states explicitly where it agrees with, and where it goes beyond, that work.

The computational results were obtained with the authors' own Python
