We submit the enclosed regular paper, "Joint Code-Dimension and k-Galois-Hull
Enumerators for Repeated-Root Constacyclic Codes over Square-Free Affine
Algebras", by Arun Kumar Yadav, Vipin Gupta and Manju Pruthi, for
consideration for publication in the IEEE Transactions on Information Theory.
It has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by any other
journal; all authors have approved the submission. This is a full regular paper
(a 42-page double-spaced review manuscript), not a correspondence.

WHAT THE PAPER DOES. For all A-linear lambda-constacyclic codes of a fixed
repeated-root length over a square-free affine algebra over a finite field, we
determine the exact joint distribution of the code dimension and the k-Galois
hull dimension. The Frobenius identity writes every component modulus as a
p^t-th power of a square-free kernel, so a code is an exponent selection on the
distinct kernel factors; the k-Galois dual then acts through a
Frobenius-twisted reciprocal permutation of those factors, and on each of its
orbits the hull dimension is a cyclic sum of pairwise minima of consecutive
exponents. The bivariate law of one orbit is the trace of a power of an explicit
weighted transfer matrix with p^t + 1 states, and multiplying over orbits - with
a dimension-only factor for the components whose twist is incompatible - gives a
bivariate enumerator whose coefficients are the exact multiplicities.

MAIN CONTRIBUTIONS.
(1) The exact joint enumerator, valid for every twist including incompatible
    components, with the known per-code hull formula recovered as a special case.
(2) The exact support of the attainable (code dimension, hull dimension) pairs
    on one orbit: a trapezoid, including a lower bound that is invisible from
    dimension considerations alone (Appendix A).
(3) Closed means and variances, conditional laws, and the exact numbers of LCD,
    self-orthogonal, dual-containing and self-dual codes; in the fully compatible
    case the LCD count is independent of the multiplicity.
(4) An algorithm polynomial in the length, the multiplicity and the number of
    kernel factors, verified exhaustively against finite-field linear algebra in
    characteristics 2 and 3, and evaluated for all 3^31 codes of length 62
    over F_32.

RELATION TO THE LITERATURE. For a single code, the Galois hull dimension of
constacyclic codes is known, over a field (Debnath, Prakash and Islam, 2023) and
over an affine algebra (Debnath, Islam, Martinez-Moro and Prakash, 2026), and
the average hull dimension has been computed (Debnath and Prakash, 2025). The
contribution of this paper is the full joint law over a family of codes together
with its enumerative consequences; the manuscript states explicitly where it
agrees with that work and what it adds beyond it.
